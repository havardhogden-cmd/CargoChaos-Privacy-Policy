# Privacy Policy – CargoChaos!

**Developer:** 2dSlug
**App:** CargoChaos!
**Effective date:** November 24, 2026

## 1. Introduction

2dSlug ("we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how CargoChaos! collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you play our mobile game.

CargoChaos! is intended for users of all ages, including children under the age of 13.

We aim to collect only information necessary to operate the game, provide its features, display advertisements, and support online services.

## 2. Information We Collect

CargoChaos! does not require users to create an account directly with us.

However, certain third-party services integrated into the game may collect or process information automatically.

### A. Advertising – Google AdMob

CargoChaos! uses Google AdMob to display advertisements.

Depending on the user's age, consent status, device, and applicable privacy settings, Google and its advertising services may process information such as:

- Advertising identifiers and other device identifiers.
- IP address and approximate location derived from the IP address.
- Device information, including device model, operating system, and language.
- Advertising interactions, such as impressions and clicks.
- Advertising and consent-related information.

Advertising is used to support the development and maintenance of CargoChaos!.

For more information about Google's data practices, please visit:

[https://policies.google.com/privacy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

### B. Consent Management – Google UMP

CargoChaos! uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to manage privacy choices and consent where required.

This service may process information relating to consent status and privacy preferences.

Users can access available privacy options through the game's settings menu.

### C. Google Play Games Services

CargoChaos! uses Google Play Games Services to provide leaderboard functionality.

When this service is available and authentication succeeds, Google Play Games may process information such as:

- Player ID.
- Gamer name or display name.
- Game scores and leaderboard information.
- Information needed to provide and maintain the gaming service.

Leaderboard information may be visible to other players according to the user's Google Play Games privacy settings.

Google Play Games handles this information according to Google's Privacy Policy:

[https://policies.google.com/privacy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

### D. Locally Stored Game Data

CargoChaos! stores certain game information locally on the user's device, using Android application storage.

This may include:

- Personal best scores.
- Selected boat and cargo skins.
- Unlocked cosmetic items.
- Game settings, including sound and frame rate preferences.
- Advertising cooldown information.

This information is used to provide the game's functionality and is not stored in a personal account operated by 2dSlug.

## 3. Children’s Privacy

CargoChaos! is intended for a general audience, including children under 13.

We recognize that children require additional privacy protections.

Advertising and data processing involving children must comply with applicable privacy laws and Google Play Families requirements.

We do not knowingly request personal information directly from children through registration forms, and CargoChaos! does not require users to provide their name, email address, or telephone number to play.

Third-party services, including advertising and Google Play Games, may process device or service information as described in this policy.

We do not sell children's personal information.

## 4. How Information Is Used

Information processed through CargoChaos! and its integrated services may be used to:

- Provide core game functionality.
- Maintain and display leaderboards.
- Display advertisements.
- Manage advertising consent and privacy preferences.
- Maintain advertising frequency limits.
- Improve service reliability and prevent abuse.

## 5. Sharing of Information

2dSlug does not sell personal information.

Information may be processed by the following service providers as necessary to provide the game:

**Google AdMob:** Advertising delivery and related services.

**Google UMP:** Consent and privacy management.

**Google Play Games Services:** Player authentication and leaderboard functionality.

These services may process information according to their own privacy policies and applicable agreements.

## 6. Data Storage and Retention

Game preferences and progress stored locally remain on the user's device until they are removed by clearing the application's data or uninstalling the game.

Information processed by Google services is retained according to Google's applicable retention policies and the user's Google account settings.

2dSlug does not operate its own player account database or cloud storage system for CargoChaos! at this time.

## 7. Data Security

We rely on established third-party services, including Google, for advertising, consent management, and leaderboard functionality.

Information transmitted by these services is handled according to their security practices and applicable policies.

No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## 8. Your Privacy Choices

Users may manage available privacy and advertising consent choices through the privacy options provided in CargoChaos!.

Users can also manage Google account and Google Play Games privacy settings through their Google account.

For information about Google's privacy practices, visit:

[https://policies.google.com/privacy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

## 9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the game, its services, or applicable legal requirements change.

The latest version will always be made available at the public Privacy Policy URL associated with CargoChaos!.

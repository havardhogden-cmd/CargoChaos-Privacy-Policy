# Privacy Policy – CargoChaos!

**Developer:** 2dSlug  
**App:** CargoChaos!  
**Effective date:** November 25, 2026

## 1. Introduction

2dSlug ("we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, processed, and shared when you use the mobile game CargoChaos!.

CargoChaos! is designed and marketed for users aged **16 and older**, including users aged 16–17 and adults aged 18 and above.

The game does not require users to create an account directly with 2dSlug. However, third-party services integrated into the game may automatically collect or process certain information to provide advertising, consent management, and online game features.

2dSlug does not operate its own player account database or game server.

## 2. Information We Collect and Process

### A. Advertising – Google AdMob

CargoChaos! uses Google AdMob to display advertisements.

Google Mobile Ads SDK and related Google advertising services may automatically collect and process information such as:

- Advertising identifiers and other device identifiers.
- IP address and approximate location derived from the IP address.
- Device information, including device model, operating system, and language.
- App interactions, such as app launches, taps, and advertisement views.
- Advertising interactions, including impressions and clicks.
- Diagnostics and performance information.
- Information used for advertising delivery, measurement, fraud prevention, and analytics.

This information may be collected and shared with Google and its advertising partners as necessary to provide advertising services.

The availability and personalization of advertisements may depend on applicable laws, consent choices, device settings, and advertising configurations.

For more information about Google's data practices, please visit:

[Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

### B. Consent Management – Google User Messaging Platform (UMP)

CargoChaos! uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to manage privacy choices and consent where required.

UMP may process information relating to consent status, privacy preferences, and applicable regulatory requirements.

Depending on the user's location and applicable requirements, consent messages or other privacy notices may be displayed.

Users can access available privacy options through the game's settings menu.

For more information:

[Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

### C. Google Play Games Services

CargoChaos! uses Google Play Games Services to provide global leaderboard functionality.

When Google Play Games authentication is available and succeeds, Google may process information such as:

- Player ID and gaming identity.
- Gamer name or display name.
- Game scores and leaderboard information.
- Other information necessary to provide and maintain Google Play Games functionality.

Leaderboard scores and associated player information may be visible to other players according to the leaderboard functionality and the user's Google Play Games settings.

Google Play Games processes this information according to Google's Privacy Policy:

[Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

### D. Locally Stored Game Data

CargoChaos! stores certain game information locally on the user's device using Android application storage.

This may include:

- Personal best scores.
- Selected boat and cargo skins.
- Unlocked cosmetic items.
- Game settings, including sound and frame rate preferences.
- Advertising cooldown information.

This information is used to provide the game's functionality and is not stored in a personal account operated by 2dSlug.

Local game data can generally be removed by clearing the application's data or uninstalling the game.

## 3. Age and Children's Privacy
